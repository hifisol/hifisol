## Security Advisory Retainer

A fixed monthly fee that reserves a set amount of time for your business. You're not paying per question or per hour — you have a budget of advisory time each month that you can use however you need. Unused hours don't roll over, but the predictable cost makes budgeting easy.

### Tiers

| Tier | Price | Hours/mo | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|----------|
| **Essentials** | $149/mo | 2 hrs | Monthly check-in, report translation (up to 1 report/mo), security news alerts relevant to your business, email/phone support |
| **Professional** | $349/mo | 5 hrs | Everything in Essentials + vendor security evaluations, incident guidance (business hours), quarterly security posture review, status summaries for partners/insurers |
| **Premium** | $549/mo | 10 hrs | Everything in Professional + priority response (including after-hours), bi-weekly check-ins, proactive threat briefings, compliance guidance, security policy review |
| **Enterprise** | $749/mo | 15+ hrs | Everything in Premium + dedicated advisory relationship, on-call incident support, annual strategy planning, custom reporting cadence |

### What's Included Across All Tiers

- **Monthly check-ins** — review security posture, discuss concerns, update recommendations
- **Report translation** — we take technical scan/audit reports and explain what actually matters
- **Security news briefing** — when a major breach or vulnerability hits the news, we tell you if it affects you and what to do
- **Incident guidance** — if something goes wrong, you have someone to call who knows your environment
- **Status summaries** — clear overviews for business partners, insurers, or stakeholders
